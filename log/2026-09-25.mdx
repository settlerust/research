# The quarter blend through the protocol arena

The [engine retest](../reports/tables-retest.mdx) left one candidate for the
protocol tier: `ntuple-leaf`'s search with the hand-written terms blended
into the learned tables at a quarter of their weight, +0.066 and +0.064 wins
per game at the two engine lineups for 1.4 times the decision time. It is now
registered as `ntuple-leaf-blend` (the `ntuple-leaf` command with
`"hand":0.25` inside `leaf`) and was played against `ntuple-leaf` through the
authoritative server as a swapped pair on deterministic seeds 2000 to 2019:
[half A](https://github.com/settlerust/research/blob/dev/experiments/0d174884-6e7e-4d88-8f1c-dd51cb21ba29.toml)
with the blend in slot 0,
[half B](https://github.com/settlerust/research/blob/dev/experiments/23b1085d-20a9-473f-a31d-daa907181449.toml)
with the blend in slot 1, `eta` and `fast` in the other seats, four rotations
per seed, 80 games each, the same caps as the cohort that made `ntuple-leaf`
the baseline, twelve matches in flight. A
[four-game smoke](/research/records/runs/692397a2-d408-421c-b7b6-ac518031aba4)
checked the plumbing first: every game valid, both search seats reporting
the tables evaluator on every decision.

[Half A](/research/records/runs/cfee89ba-3e06-4549-8cae-c31652464985): 34
blend wins against 33 for `ntuple-leaf`, contrast +0.013 (−0.156 to +0.181).
[Half B](/research/records/runs/2047d413-be8e-4a80-bcea-4c53fbf78619): 34
against 32, contrast −0.025 (−0.195 to +0.145) for `ntuple-leaf` minus the
blend. Both halves completed all 80 games with no timeout moves. The
seating-corrected effect of the blend is +0.019 wins per game (95% interval
−0.087 to +0.124) and the seating term −0.006 (−0.139 to +0.126). By the
registered rule this is inconclusive: the interval includes zero and the
estimate is below +0.03, but the interval also covers the engine estimate,
so twenty boards in both seatings could not separate an effect of the
engine's size from nothing. `ntuple-leaf` stays the protocol reference and
the server default is unchanged. The
[report](../reports/blend-protocol.mdx) has both Wilson tables, the play
statistics, and the decision traces.

Under the 1.5 s clock on a machine shared with five other agents (load
average 60 to 80 on 32 cores), the blend completed depth 3 in 49.9% and
51.8% of contested main-phase decisions against 61.2% and 60.9% for the
tables alone, and was stopped by the budget in a quarter of them against a
fifth, at about 1.1 times the control's time per contested decision (932
and 911 ms against 827 and 834 ms). Its cost shows up as lost depth rather
than as the engine's 1.4 times wall time. As in the engine, the blend built
more cities and finished with about half a point more per game; the points
did not become wins.

The first attempt at half A
([registered protocol](https://github.com/settlerust/research/blob/dev/experiments/7de5ddb4-c007-4e66-b4e6-7c08e0fa07c1.toml),
[abandoned attempt](/research/records/runs/99bfec6f-10ac-4536-99bf-460d62d0fea5))
stopped at its nineteenth game: the harness's seat join at game creation was
refused with a version conflict before any policy process started, so the
game is invalid and the schedule stopped as registered. Eighteen games had
completed validly (blend 6 wins, `ntuple-leaf` 4, `eta` 3, `fast` 5); they
are recorded and not pooled. The server checks a command's version before
it recognizes a duplicate request, so a join envelope retried after a lost
response under load fails with a conflict although the seat was taken;
`harness/arena.py` now refreshes the snapshot, keeps a seat it already
holds, and retries a join on conflict, as the ready and start steps already
did. Half A was registered again with the same text and half B ran as
first registered. Both searches also logged a few
`continuation could not reach the next-turn horizon` fallbacks, a
pre-existing search path that plays the first legal action when the search
returns no candidate: 22 and 19 for the blend, 19 and 27 for the tables
alone, out of 9,000 to 10,000 decisions per seat per half.

To read the searches' cost through the protocol, the `remote` runner's
expectimax seat gained an opt-in trace (`SETTLERS_SEARCH_TRACE`, one JSON
line per decision on stderr with the evaluator, the completed depth, and the
search and wall times; server `crates/policies/src/expectimax.rs`, off by
default and never changing a decision). The harness keeps it in each seat's
private policy log; `analysis/blend_protocol.py` aggregates it together
with the two halves' per-seed contrasts and the public tapes. Study
`patterns/blend-protocol` is complete. Resolving an effect of the engine's
size through the protocol would take a pair of about 64-board halves (512
games, roughly ±0.06), preferably on an unloaded machine so the blend keeps
its depth; the cheaper question is whether a subset of the hand-written
terms buys the engine gain without the depth cost.
