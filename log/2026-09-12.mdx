# Every search switch under the learned leaf

Study `log/tables-retest`. The ten studies launched on 2026-09-11 to retest the
search's switches under the learned tables finished today; nine filed complete
studies and the browser-budget study was stopped by the user before its
registered stages, with its work kept on the `research/browser-budget` branches.
`analysis/tables_retest.py` rebuilds every swapped pair of the nine studies from
the registered experiments and the archived runs, orients each effect so that a
positive value favours the candidate, and pools each switch per leaf and lineup
after the fact: 145 pairs and 74,240 games. Every study's reported
outcome was checked against that recomputation and matched.

What holds: bargaining (counters off −0.094 at the builder table and −0.119 at
the table of searches; the whole package −0.165 and −0.098), and the search's
information inputs (four dice scenarios −0.392, one sampled world −0.109,
public-count beliefs −0.143, no proposals −0.172). What improves the player at
both tables: the hand-written terms blended into the tables at a quarter of their
weight, +0.066 and +0.064, at 1.4 times the decision time. Eight sampled worlds
(+0.045) and the search-ranked opening (+0.061, carried by the reused
development boards) read positive at the builder table only. The robber, card
timing, hand, road, prediction, endgame, and planner-weight switches show no
effect that survives both tables. The [synthesis report](../reports/tables-retest.mdx)
has the full table and the browser recommendation: keep the current browser
configuration, and use the quarter blend if the tables reach the browser, after a
protocol cohort.

Verification: server dev passes formatting, clippy with warnings as errors, and
all 183 workspace tests after every merge of the program. Failures during the
program: two Claude Fable subagents stopped on usage limits and were finished by
Claude Opus 5 agents from their saved state; one opencode session ended on a
provider error and resumed; two agents slept through finished queues and were
woken.
