# The first ten-game LLM arena comparison

Study `log/llm-arena` documents the completed arena series
`baseten-series-2026-09-17`. The [report](../reports/llm-arena.mdx) introduces the
TypeScript tool loop, seated information boundary, search, memory, and Parquet
recordings. The new competition exhibit preserves all ten round IDs, exact
model identities, seat assignments, opening and regular turn orders, final
outcomes, and replay hashes.

DeepSeek V4 Pro won five games, Kimi K3 three, GLM 5.3 two, and Inkling none.
All ten reached a recorded winner; no game was excluded or replaced. There were
tool errors, provider failures, compaction fallbacks, and engine timeouts within
completed games. Inkling's action-format failures and DeepSeek's higher timeout
count limit an interpretation based solely on strategy.

The lineup, schedule, settings and runtime were frozen before play. This study
files an exploratory comparison retrospectively, not a preregistered causal
hypothesis. The standalone learned-search baseline remains unchanged. No new
inference was run to publish this report.

Attribution: Codex implementation and audit, with `gpt-5.6-luna` reviewing
transcripts. The player IDs are recorded separately and exactly in the exhibit.


## Expert baseline follow-up

The original comparison has been retired from the public report. Its ten full
recordings, original report and metadata were archived without changing their
hashes. The original arena journals and frozen runtime remain available locally.
Inkling’s malformed tool calls and poor trade decisions made it an unsuitable
member of a strength comparison. Its rounds are not pooled with the new study.

Study `log/llm-expert-competition` owns the replacement report. The
[registered protocol](../experiments/981f6ecf-d016-4698-a14e-35bf1ed8876d.toml)
freezes ten distinct boards, the three LLMs, the Expert baseline, a two-minute
turn ceiling and the complete token/search settings before paid inference.
The source runtime and schedule digests are in the registration. The hypothesis
is that at least one LLM wins more games than Expert; equality does not support
it. This small comparison is descriptive, with uncertainty reported.

Before registration, an Expert smoke exposed unnecessary worker termination
between completed decisions. Preserving idle workers retains their loaded
tables; the short real game now completes. All 30 arena tests and checkpoint
verification passed before launch. No smoke results enter the ten-game total.
Published replays omit repeated input prompts, images and opaque provider
payloads while retaining every move, reasoning response, scratchpad revision
and timeline event. Full source archives remain local.

Attribution: Codex implementation and analysis. No transcript subagent was used
for this follow-up. The batch is still running; final interpretation follows
after every scheduled game has been audited.


## Restart with spatial text inputs

The user explicitly stopped the first Expert-baseline attempt while two games
were running. They ended at turns 37 and 31, with no winner. Eight
scheduled games never started. The [interruption record](../records/runs/eee33ac0-bbae-4f02-923b-b3c5158c96b8.md)
retains the original protocol and hash-verified local archive; those games are
excluded from the replacement denominator because the input protocol changed,
not because of who was leading.

The [replacement protocol](../experiments/6fd3b0cd-e44f-4b6f-8725-26f94dbf3c56.toml) freezes ten new boards and
the same models, seating balance and budgets. Every LLM receives labelled text,
a stable ASCII hex map, named resource counts and contextual action rankings.
Tool receipts, scratchpad contents and compaction transcripts are text. Typed
JSON remains at the provider transport and outgoing tool-call boundaries.
Images remain available for Kimi and GLM. Thirty-three arena tests and all eight
vendored-file/checkpoint checks passed before the new runtime was frozen.

The series `kimi-glm-deepseek-expert-text-2026-09-17` is running. No strength
conclusion is available yet. Attribution: Codex implementation and audit.
