Usage sums provider-returned response totals, including off-turn and compaction
work. Reasoning is included in generated tokens. Failed and cancelled calls
with unknown usage are not fabricated; their budget reservations are not actual
reported consumption. Decision latency pools completed active and spectate
responses and excludes failed requests and compaction, so the median does not
describe the timeout tail.

There were 15 output-limit finishes on DeepSeek active decision responses, 43
on its off-turn decision responses and 22 on its compaction responses. Kimi
recorded 48 decision HTTP 500 errors, DeepSeek four and GLM one. These are
reliability confounds, not evidence of inferior strategic understanding alone.
The 73 tool errors include 37 expired activations, ten aborted operations and
five schema-validation errors, all five from DeepSeek. Other failures include
illegal victims, insufficient resources and races over an already closed trade.
Engine timeouts, request errors and tool errors are separate, overlapping counts.
