# The frozen candidate through the protocol arena

<ResearchCallout title="Finding · positive direction, rule not met">
Run `e4949ebe-8132-4428-aae9-37ae1e0c3748` completed **80 of 80** games through
the authoritative server with no timeout moves. `expectimax-v2-deep` won
**31 (38.8%)**; the ETA builder in the compared slot won **25 (31.2%)**. The
registered rule asked for a 10-point gap with a one-sided exact test at
p ≤ 0.05; the gap is 7.5 points and p = 0.25. The cohort is inconclusive.
</ResearchCallout>

<ResearchChart asset="expectimax-v2-protocol-wins" />

The lineup was the frozen depth-3 candidate (1.5 s budget, sixteen scenarios,
eight sampled worlds), two ETA builders and one fast builder, rotated through
every seat over 20 rounds. Twelve matches ran at once against one server, so
the candidate's decisions shared the machine with eleven other searches; its
budget is a wall clock, so contention makes it search less, never longer.

The other ETA slot won 13 games and the fast builder 11. Two identical ETA
policies finishing 12 games apart is a reminder of how far unpaired 80-game
cohorts move; the [engine arena](engine-arena-calibration.mdx) pairs boards to
take that variance out of development comparisons.

This candidate was frozen before a turn-planning defect was found in the search
(its inner turns collapsed to ending the turn, and non-ending root actions were
valued without the opponents' round). The result stands as recorded for that
exact binary and configuration; the corrected search is a new candidate and
will have its own cohorts.
