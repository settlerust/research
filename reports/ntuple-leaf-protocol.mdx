# The learned leaf through the protocol arena

<ResearchCallout title="Finding · the tables beat the protocol baseline, and become it">
Through the authoritative server, on 20 deterministic seeds each played once
per rotation of the four slots, `ntuple-leaf` (the `expectimax-v2-plan` search
at depth 3 under 1.5 s with the learned hex plus portfolio tables as its leaf)
won **46 of 80 games (57.5%)**; `expectimax-v2-plan` itself won **25
(31.2%)**, the ETA builder 5 and the fast builder 4. The paired per-seed
contrast is **+0.263 wins per game** (95% interval +0.094 to +0.431). All 80
games completed validly with no timeout move, so the registered rule is met
and `ntuple-leaf` is the new protocol-arena baseline.
</ResearchCallout>

## What was registered

Experiment `a5e9b3f1-8ace-418f-b8e8-bd54c825c5c9` was registered under
`patterns/ntuple-leaf` before the candidate was frozen: the frozen tables at
`artifacts/ntuple/hex-portfolio-main.bin` as the leaf of the baseline's exact
search configuration (depth 3, 1.5 s budget, 8 scenarios, 4 sampled worlds) in
slot 0, `expectimax-v2-plan` in slot 1, `eta` and `fast` in the other seats, on
seeds 320 to 339 in deterministic mode, 80 games. The rule: all games complete
validly with no server timeout moves; support only if the 95% interval of the
paired per-seed win difference between slots 0 and 1 lies above zero.

Run `e861616e-8100-43e6-a8d3-39047b5b6669` played all 80 with twelve matches in
flight. The two searches ran as separate `remote` processes against the server
and saw only their own seated view and event history; the tables were loaded
from the file named in the policy registry, whose SHA-256
(`f8e61e66…`) is recorded with the candidate's receipt. Games averaged 73
turns.

| Slot / policy | Wins | Completed | Win rate | 95% Wilson interval | Mean points |

| 0 / `ntuple-leaf` | 46 | 80 | 57.5% | 46.6% to 67.7% | 8.28 |

The result agrees with the [engine-arena screens](ntuple-leaf.mdx): at depth 3
against depth 3 on 64 fresh engine seeds the contrast was +0.242, and here
through the protocol on 20 seeds it is +0.263.

## Limitations

One lineup, one seat rotation scheme, and twenty seeds. The interval is wide
because the cohort is small; the engine screens on more seeds carry the
sharper estimate. Both searches negotiate only with the fixed builders'
willingness model, so nothing here speaks to trade-rich tables with
negotiating opponents or to play against humans. The tables decide more slowly
than the hand-written leaf under the same budget, which the 1.5 s cap absorbs
at depth 3 but which would matter under a tighter deadline.
