# Hand management under the learned leaf

Spending down and removing the hand-risk term also remain inconclusive. The learned leaf already discards about half as often as the hand-written leaf.

## The arms

The baseline seat is the arena's tables configuration,
`v2:{"depth":2,"leaf":{"tables":".../hex-portfolio-main.bin"}}`, and each
candidate adds one switch. `spend_down` and `discard_projection` act on the
candidate lists, so they run under the tables alone. `spend_down` offers up to
three bank trades and two roads chosen only to shed cards when the hand is over
the discard limit; `discard_projection` ranks each legal discard by the build
deficits of the kept hand plus the production expected before the next own
turn.

The third arm is the leaf's seven-risk pricing, `weights.hand_risk` 0 against
the default 1. A leaf term is inert under the tables alone (`leaf.hand` 0 skips
the hand-written terms), so it must be measured on a blend. The program first
registered it on the full blend (`hand` 1.0 in both seats), then a program-wide
screen measured the full blend itself at −0.207 wins per game against the
tables alone (95% interval −0.284 to −0.130) while the quarter blend read
+0.074 (+0.011 to +0.138). A switch measured on a configuration that much
weaker than the baseline says little about anything anyone would run, so the
six full-blend registrations were withdrawn before running and the arm was
re-registered on the quarter blend (`hand` 0.25 in both seats). The withdrawn
registrations are listed in the AgentContext.

## Design

Every contrast is a swapped pair on the same seeds: half A seats the candidate
in slot 0 and the control in slot 1, half B swaps them, and the effect is the
per-seed half-difference of the two slot-0-minus-slot-1 contrasts (the seating
term is their half-sum), computed by `analysis/seating_pair.py` from the
retained games. Each half is 64 seeds played once per rotation of the four
slots, 256 games, deterministic. The builders lineup puts an ETA and a fast
builder in slots 2 and 3, matching the hand-leaf cohorts; the population lineup
puts the tables baseline seat in slot 2 and ETA in slot 3, so three searches
sit at the table. Stages: development on seeds 0 to 63, confirmation on seeds
800 to 863 (unused by any earlier experiment), population on seeds 800 to 863,
and an extension on seeds 864 to 927 in both lineups only when the pooled
builders-lineup effect over 128 seeds is positive with its interval's lower
bound within 0.03 of zero. The registered decision rule for every pair: the
seating-corrected effect's 95% seed interval excludes zero in the hypothesized
direction (support), excludes it the other way (refutation), or neither
(inconclusive). Pooled estimates across stages are after-the-fact pooling.

All 22 cohorts completed 256 of 256 games. One earlier attempt at the first
cohort was interrupted after 3 games by an operator mistake (the queue driver
was not session-detached) and is filed as interrupted; its games were not
pooled and the cohort was rerun from scratch.

## The mechanisms fire, and it does not matter

Per-game means for the candidate against the control, pooled over each arm's
stages. Hand size at the roll is the arena's new per-seat counter, the hand
summed over the game's rolls before each roll's production and discards.

| Arm | Cards discarded | Turns over the limit | Times robbed | Bank trades | Hand at the roll | Decision ms |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `spend_down` | 4.69 / 5.12 | 0.40 / 0.47 | 3.75 / 3.78 | 4.63 / 4.44 | 4.80 / 4.80 | 814 / 682 |
| `discard_projection` | 5.03 / 4.98 | 0.45 / 0.45 | 3.87 / 3.89 | 4.49 / 4.53 | 4.79 / 4.80 | 615 / 616 |
| `hand_risk` 0 (blend) | 6.09 / 5.96 | 0.56 / 0.54 | 4.14 / 4.18 | 4.84 / 4.89 | 4.88 / 4.85 | 855 / 866 |

The switches move the counts they target. Spending down ends fewer turns over
the limit (0.40 against 0.47) and discards less; removing the risk term on the
blend raises discards a little; the projection changes which cards go without
changing the counts. None of it moves the win rate.

The comparison with the hand-written leaf explains why. Under that leaf the
control discarded 8.6 to 9.2 cards per game and ended about 1.0 turn per game
over the limit; under the tables the control discards about 5 and ends 0.4 to
0.5 turns over the limit, holding 4.8 cards at the roll. The tables were
trained by self-play with the same fixed discard rule, so they already value a
held card by what it becomes, and the play they induce rarely reaches eight
cards. The situation all three mechanisms address comes up half as often, and
the earlier study had already shown pricing it differently buys nothing at this
depth even when it does come up: under the hand-written leaf on the same
development seeds, `spend_down` read +0.066 in one seating (the size of the
seating term), `discard_projection` corrected to −0.029 (−0.073 to +0.015),
and the `hand_risk` sweep over 0, 2, and 4 was flat
([hand-management report](hand-management.mdx)).

## What could still be wrong

Everything here is engine-arena development tier at depth 2 on deterministic
boards, with two fixed lineups. The projection's builders-lineup intervals do
not exclude a gain under +0.034, so a small effect there is possible; the
population lineup and the pooled 320-seed reading make anything at or above the
+0.03 browser threshold unlikely. The blend the risk arm sits on was chosen by
a screen, and the quarter strength also quarters the term's influence, so the
arm answers "does the term matter at the strength anyone would run it" rather
than "does seven-risk pricing matter at full strength"; at full strength under
the hand-written leaf the sweep was already flat. Decision times were measured
on a heavily shared machine, so the absolute milliseconds are inflated and only
the within-run ratios carry meaning. The seating terms these pairs measured
(+0.03 to +0.12) match the null pairs in the seating report, so the correction
is behaving as expected. Finally, the tables were trained with the fixed
discard rule; a leaf trained with a different rule could price these situations
differently.

## What this means for the browser

The browser build runs the hand-written leaf at depth 3 under a one-second
budget, and the program's rule for adopting a switch there is a pooled effect
of at least +0.03 wins per game with the interval excluding zero and a decision
cost within 25% of the control's. No arm comes close. `spend_down` is at best
neutral under the tables, leans negative at a search-heavy table, and costs
19% more decision time; under the hand-written leaf its only reading was the
seating term. `discard_projection` is free and never negative, but its best
pooled reading is +0.012 and the population lineup caps it at +0.019. The
`hand_risk` weight was flat across 0, 1, 2, and 4 under the hand-written leaf
the browser actually runs, and removing it on the blend under the tables is
worth −0.001. Enable nothing; keep `hand_risk` at 1.

<AgentContext title="Detailed result and cohort context">

Three mechanisms from the hand-written-leaf study were re-registered as swapped
pairs with the learned n-tuple tables as the leaf, 512 games per stage per arm
on deterministic boards. Pooled after the fixed stages, `spend_down` reads
**−0.020 wins per game** over 192 seeds (95% interval −0.049 to +0.010) and
costs 19% more decision time, `discard_projection` reads **+0.006** over 320
seeds (−0.009 to +0.022) at no measurable cost, and removing the `hand_risk`
term on the quarter blend reads **−0.001** over 192 seeds (−0.029 to +0.028).
Every per-stage interval crosses zero and no arm comes close to the +0.03
browser threshold. The defaults stay: both switches off, `hand_risk` at 1.

</AgentContext>
